# Counterpart Exclusives Mod

A gameplay mod for Pokémon (Generation 1, 2, and 3) running on the Pokémon Love2D engine.

## Overview
In traditional Pokémon games, certain wild species are exclusive to one version of a release pair (e.g. Vulpix in LeafGreen vs Growlithe in FireRed; Lotad in Sapphire vs Seedot in Ruby).

This mod makes counterpart version-exclusive species available in all versions of each generation family:
- **FireRed & LeafGreen:** Ekans/Sandshrew, Oddish/Bellsprout, Psyduck/Slowpoke, Growlithe/Vulpix, Shellder/Staryu, Scyther/Pinsir, Electabuzz/Magmar, Wooper/Marill, Murkrow/Misdreavus, Qwilfish/Remoraid, Delibird/Mantine, Skarmory.
- **Ruby, Sapphire & Emerald:** Seedot/Lotad, Mawile/Sableye, Zangoose/Seviper, Solrock/Lunatone.
- **Red & Blue (Gen 1):** Ekans/Sandshrew, Oddish/Bellsprout, Mankey/Meowth, Growlithe/Vulpix, Scyther/Pinsir, Electabuzz/Magmar.
- **Gold & Silver (Gen 2):** Spinarak/Ledyba, Vulpix/Growlithe, Mankey/Meowth, Gligar/Delibird, Mantine/Skarmory, Teddiursa/Phanpy.

The mod shares encounter probability between counterpart pairs on the exact routes and biomes where they naturally occur, preserving original level ranges, encounter rates, and story progression. It covers walking, surfing and fishing encounters; on Gen 3, Sweet Scent and Rock Smash encounters keep their original species.

## Installation
1. Download `counterpart_exclusives-0.1.0.zip` from the releases page.
2. Place or extract the mod into your `mods/` directory.
3. Start the game. Counterpart wild Pokémon will appear in the wild immediately.

## Save Safety & Rollback
This mod operates entirely in memory during encounter generation. It does not alter your save files, Pokédex progress flags, or badge flags. Disabling the mod restores original version-exclusive wild tables immediately.
